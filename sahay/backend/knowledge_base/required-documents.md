## Mandatory documents
Every applicant must upload:
1. Recent passport-size photograph (JPG, 20–50 KB).
2. Mark sheet of the qualifying examination (Class 12 or bachelor's degree).
3. Family income certificate for financial year 2025–26 issued by the Tehsildar or another competent revenue authority. Self-declarations are not accepted.
4. Proof of admission for 2026–27: fee receipt or bonafide certificate signed by the college principal.
5. Bank passbook first page or a cancelled cheque showing the applicant's own name, account number and IFSC. Joint accounts with a parent are allowed.
6. Government photo ID of the applicant.

## Conditional documents
- Caste certificate issued by the competent authority, only if applying under the SC, ST or OBC-NCL limit. The OBC certificate must show non-creamy layer status and be issued after 1 April 2025.
- Disability certificate (UDID card) showing at least 40% disability, only if applying under the disability limit.

## File format and size rules
All documents other than the photograph must be PDF files of at most 200 KB each. Scans must be clear and in colour. Password-protected PDFs are rejected. Each document must be self-attested (signed by the applicant) before scanning.

## Common reasons applications are rejected
- Income certificate from the wrong financial year or a self-declaration.
- Bank account that is inactive or not linked to the applicant's name.
- Blurred or incomplete scans.
- Name on documents does not match the name in the application. Use exactly the name on the Class 12 mark sheet.
