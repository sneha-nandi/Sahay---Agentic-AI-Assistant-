## Purpose of the scheme
The Demo Merit Scholarship supports students from low-income families who are enrolled in a full-time undergraduate or postgraduate course at a recognised college or university. Selected students receive ₹50,000 per academic year for tuition and ₹10,000 per year for books.

## Eligibility criteria
An applicant is eligible only if all of the following are true:
- The applicant is enrolled in the first year of a full-time undergraduate or postgraduate course in academic year 2026–27.
- The applicant scored at least 75% in the qualifying examination (Class 12 for undergraduate, bachelor's degree for postgraduate). The limit is 65% for applicants from reserved categories (SC, ST, OBC-NCL) and persons with disabilities.
- The total annual family income from all sources is ₹4,50,000 or less for financial year 2025–26.
- The applicant is not receiving any other government scholarship for the same course.
Students in distance-learning or part-time courses are not eligible.

## Important dates and deadlines
- Application window opens: 1 September 2026.
- Last date to submit the online application: 31 October 2026, 11:59 PM IST.
- Last date for the college to verify the application: 15 November 2026.
- Correction window (only for applications already submitted): 1–7 November 2026.
- Results announced: by 15 December 2026.
Applications cannot be submitted or edited after the deadlines. No extensions are given for delays caused by the applicant.

## Renewal
Scholarships are renewed each year if the student passes the annual examination with at least 60% marks and attendance of at least 75%. Renewal applications open on 1 August each year.
