## Before you start
Keep scanned copies of all documents from the document checklist ready. Use a working mobile number and email address; all updates are sent there. The session times out after 20 minutes of inactivity, and unsaved data is lost.

## Step-by-step application process
1. Open the application form on the portal.
2. Enter personal details exactly as they appear on the Class 12 mark sheet.
3. Enter contact details: email, mobile number and full postal address with PIN code.
4. Select your category and enter your qualifying examination percentage and annual family income.
5. Enter your bank account details. Double-check the account number and IFSC; the scholarship is paid only to this account.
6. Upload the required documents.
7. Read the declaration carefully and tick the checkbox only if every statement is true.
8. Review all details and click Submit Application.
9. Note the application reference number shown after submission and save the acknowledgement.

## After submission
The application goes to your college for verification. You can track its status using the reference number. Details can only be changed during the correction window. Submitting false information leads to cancellation of the scholarship and recovery of any amount paid.

## Application fee
There is no application fee for the Demo Merit Scholarship. Any page or person asking for payment to process the application is not official.
