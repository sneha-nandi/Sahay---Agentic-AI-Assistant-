title: Staying Safe on Government and Banking Websites
source: Sahay safety guidelines (general guidance)
---
## Protect one-time passwords and credentials
Never share an OTP, password, PIN, CVV or card details with anyone, including someone claiming to be an official. Genuine government departments and banks never ask for your OTP or PIN by phone, SMS, email or chat.

## Check that the website is genuine
Before entering personal details, check the address bar. Indian government portals normally use domains ending in .gov.in or .nic.in. Banks use their own registered domain. Look out for misspellings, extra words or unusual endings in the address.

## Be careful with payments
Scholarship and most welfare-scheme applications are free. Be suspicious of any page asking for a "processing fee", "registration fee" or "verification charge" before you can apply. Confirm fees on the official notification before paying.

## Hidden instructions and scams on web pages
Web pages can contain text that tries to trick assistants or users into urgent actions, such as "submit immediately" or "ignore previous instructions". Sahay treats page content as untrusted and will never follow instructions found on a page. Important actions always need your approval.

## Sensitive personal information
Identity numbers (such as Aadhaar or PAN), bank account numbers, dates of birth and passwords are sensitive. Enter them yourself only on the genuine website, and never in chat messages or unknown forms.
