title: Demo Merit Scholarship 2026 — Frequently Asked Questions
source: Demo Department of Higher Education, FAQ page (sample document)
---
## Can I apply if I am in the second year of my course?
No. New applications are only for students in the first year in 2026–27. Students already receiving the scholarship should apply for renewal instead.

## My family income is slightly above ₹4,50,000. Can I still apply?
No. The income limit is strict and is checked against the income certificate for financial year 2025–26.

## Can I use my parent's bank account?
Only a joint account where the applicant is one of the holders is allowed. An account in only a parent's name is not accepted.

## I made a mistake after submitting. What should I do?
Use the correction window from 1 to 7 November 2026. Outside that window details cannot be changed.

## Is Aadhaar mandatory?
You must upload one government photo ID. Aadhaar is accepted but not mandatory; a voter ID, passport or PAN card is also accepted.

## What happens if I miss the deadline of 31 October 2026?
Late applications are not accepted. You can apply in the next cycle if you are still eligible.

## Who verifies my application?
Your college's nodal officer verifies your application by 15 November 2026, then the department's district office checks it.
