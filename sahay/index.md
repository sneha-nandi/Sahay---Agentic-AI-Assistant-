Sahay – Safe Web Assistant Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 8, 2026
Sahay – Safe Web Assistant (“Sahay”, “we”, “our”, or “the extension”) is an AI-powered browser extension designed to help users understand and safely interact with complex websites, including government, banking, education, and public-service websites.
This Privacy Policy explains what information Sahay processes, why it is processed, and how users remain in control of their information.
1. Information Sahay Processes
Depending on how Sahay is used, the extension may process:
Webpage information
When a user asks Sahay to help with a webpage, Sahay may read information that is visible on the current webpage, including:
- The webpage URL
- Page title
- Visible webpage text
- Information about visible buttons, links, forms, and other webpage elements
- Information needed to understand the structure of the webpage
Sahay is designed to avoid sending the actual values entered into form fields to the AI model as part of the normal page-understanding process.
User questions and conversations
Sahay processes:
- Questions entered by the user
- Previous questions and Sahay responses needed to answer follow-up questions
- Information provided by the user when responding to clarification questions
Optional profile information
Sahay allows users to save optional profile information to make certain non-sensitive form-filling tasks easier.
Users should not enter passwords, one-time passwords (OTPs), PINs, payment-card numbers, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information into the Sahay profile.
2. How Information Is Used
Information processed by Sahay is used to:
- Understand the user's request
- Explain webpage content
- Answer questions about the current webpage
- Retrieve relevant information from Sahay's trusted knowledge sources
- Identify possible actions on a webpage
- Assess the risk of proposed browser actions
- Help the user complete permitted browser actions
- Verify whether an action was completed successfully
- Maintain conversations and provide follow-up assistance
Sahay is designed to keep the user in control of important browser actions. Certain actions require user approval before they are performed.
3. AI Processing
Sahay uses an AI model to analyze requests and webpage information and generate responses.
In the current implementation, AI processing is performed using the Google Gemini API through the Sahay backend.
Information sent to the AI service is limited to information needed for the requested Sahay functionality. Sahay's safety layer is designed to prevent passwords, OTPs, PINs, card numbers, and government identification numbers from being requested or intentionally handled by the AI model.
4. Backend Processing
Sahay communicates with a backend service to perform its AI and agentic processing.
Depending on the backend configuration, information sent to the backend may include:
- The user's question
- Relevant webpage information
- Conversation context required for a follow-up question
- Optional non-sensitive profile information
- Information required to process an approved browser action
The backend is also used to perform safety checks, retrieve trusted information, and coordinate Sahay's AI workflow.
5. Local Browser Storage
Sahay stores certain information locally in the user's browser, including:
- User preferences
- Optional profile information
- Saved conversations and conversation history
This information is stored using the browser's local extension storage.
Users can delete saved conversations using Sahay's conversation-history controls.
6. Logs and Audit Information
The Sahay backend may maintain technical audit information about processing runs, such as:
- Request and processing events
- Webpage URL and title
- AI-generated responses
- Proposed or performed actions
- Safety decisions
- Processing results and errors
Audit information is used to support debugging, safety verification, and operation of the Sahay service.
7. Information Sahay Does Not Intentionally Request
Sahay is designed not to request or handle the following sensitive information:
- Passwords
- OTPs
- PINs
- Payment-card numbers
- Government identification numbers
Users should nevertheless avoid entering sensitive information into Sahay unless absolutely necessary.
Sahay does not need access to passwords or payment credentials to provide its intended functionality.
8. User Control
Users remain in control of their browsing actions.
Sahay may suggest or prepare an action, but sensitive or consequential browser actions can require explicit user approval before execution.
Users can also:
- Delete saved conversations
- Remove optional profile information
- Stop using Sahay at any time
- Disable or uninstall the extension
9. Data Sharing
Sahay does not sell users' information.
Information may be transmitted to the Sahay backend and, where required for AI processing, to the configured AI service provider.
Sahay only transmits information necessary to provide the functionality requested by the user.
10. Data Retention
Sahay's browser-side conversation history remains stored locally until the user deletes it or removes the extension/browser data.
Backend audit information may be retained for operational, debugging, and safety purposes.
If Sahay is operated through a remote backend, the retention period may depend on the configuration and hosting environment of that backend.
11. Security
Sahay incorporates safety mechanisms designed to reduce the risk of unintended browser actions and to protect sensitive information.
However, no software or internet service can guarantee absolute security. Users should exercise caution when using Sahay on websites containing sensitive personal, financial, or authentication information.
12. Third-Party Services
Sahay may use third-party services required for AI processing, including Google Gemini.
Those services may process information transmitted to them according to their applicable terms and privacy policies.
13. Changes to This Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated when Sahay's functionality, data processing practices, or third-party services change.
The updated version will be published at the same Privacy Policy location.
